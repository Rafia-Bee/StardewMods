# Architect

Villagers sometimes want a hand making their home feel new again. With Architect, a villager can post a job on the help-wanted board asking you to redecorate their place.

## How it works

- Accept the job from the board. The villager hands you a budget of gold right away to shop with.
- The job lists what they want, like a couple of new lights, a rug, or a chair.
- Go buy furniture (Robin's shop, the Furniture Catalogue, wherever) and place it inside that villager's home.
- As you set each matching piece down, the budget counts down by what it cost. You can check the list and the leftover budget any time in your quest log or the Quest Journal.

## The money

The budget is the villager's money, not yours, so the goal is to decorate without spending more than they gave you.

- Spend less than the budget and the leftover goes back to the villager when you finish. It costs you nothing.
- Spend more than the budget and you cover the difference out of your own pocket. Just the difference, nothing extra.

## The reward

When the job is done, the villager thanks you with a handful of their favorite things, five gifts they personally love (and a few they like, if they don't have five loved ones). Handy for keeping friendships warm.

## Requirements

- More Quests Framework
- Build Placement Unlocker (this is what lets you place furniture inside a villager's house at all)

Both are required. You can tweak the budget, deadlines, how often the job shows up, and more by editing config.json (it's created the first time the game runs with the mod).
