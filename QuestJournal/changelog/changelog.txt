1.1.1
- Fixed the pinned quest box on screen. If you changed your UI scale away from 100%, clicking a pinned quest could open the wrong one or do nothing. Clicks now land on the quest you actually clicked at any UI scale.
- The "days left" line now tells you the real deadline. A quest or special order is removed the morning after its last day, so the journal now says "Final day!" on the day you have to finish it, "Due tomorrow!" when the deadline is the next day, and counts down the real days after that. Heads up: this reads one lower than the vanilla number on purpose (the game's "2 days" is really "due tomorrow"), because that's how much time you actually have. Works the same for regular quests and special orders now.

1.1.0
- Added a journal icon for Iconic Framework. Click it to open the journal.
- Works with a controller now. If you have Star Control, the journal shows up in its radial menu so you can open it with your gamepad.
- Switch between tabs with a controller using the shoulder buttons (LB/RB) or the triggers (LT/RT).
- Edit one of your own custom tabs with a controller: select it, then press Y to open its editor.
- Make a new custom tab with a controller by pressing X.
- Open a pinned quest from the HUD with a controller: point at it with the right stick and press A.
- The controller buttons (tab switching and edit) can be rebound in the settings.
Translators: see [url=https://github.com/Rafia-Bee/StardewMods/compare/QuestJournal-v1.0.0...QuestJournal-v1.1.0]i18n changes here[/url] (look at i18n/default.json).

1.0.0
- First release.
- Replaces the plain quest log with a full journal: a quest list on the left, all the details in the middle, and action buttons on the right.
- Cancel or postpone a quest right from the journal. There's also an optional Complete button (off by default) that finishes a quest and pays its reward.
- Multi-step quests from More Quests Framework show each step and how far along it is.
- Special Orders get their own tab.
- Pin quests to a top-right corner of the screen so you can see your current goal while you play. Click a pin to jump straight to that quest.
- Make your own tabs that filter quests by title, source, category, or kind. Saved per save file.
- Search bar at the top to filter the quest list by title.
- Optional warp helper warps you next to a quest's NPC.
- Optional item helper hands you the item a quest asks for.
- Resize the whole window in the settings.
- Open it with F6 or a tab in the Esc menu. Needs StardewUI. More Quests Framework and Generic Mod Config Menu are optional.
