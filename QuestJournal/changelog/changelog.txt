1.5.0
- New: it's easier to see and manage your pinned quests. Pinned quests now show a little pushpin next to their name in the list, and they float to the top so they're quick to find (there's an on/off setting for the float if you'd rather keep the normal order). There's also a key to pin or unpin (P by default): in the journal it pins the quest you have selected, and while you're playing you can point at a pinned quest in the on-screen box and press it to unpin that one. And if you want a quick way to show or hide the on-screen box, there's a key for that too, but you'll need to set it yourself in the settings first.
- New: the "complete this quest" cheat button now works on special orders too. If you have the complete cheat turned on, open the Special Orders tab, pick an order, and there's a Complete button that finishes the whole thing for you. For orders that ask you to drop items in a box, it counts as done without you having to walk over and use the box, and you get to keep your items. If the order pays gold, claim it the same way you always do.
- New: you can hide the Completed and All tabs. If you only care about the quests you're working on, the extra tabs can feel like clutter. There are two new on/off settings ("Show the Completed tab" and "Show the All tab") under General. Turn either one off and that tab disappears from the journal the next time you open it. Both are on to start, so nothing changes unless you want it to.
- New: other mods can now add their own to-dos to the journal. If a mod you use adds a task or reminder, it can show up in the journal right next to your quests, and you can pin it to the corner of the screen like anything else. The mod that adds it can give you a button to mark it done or remove it, and can even put its tasks in their own tab. You won't see anything new unless you have a mod that uses this.
- New: long lines in the on-screen pinned box no longer spill past the edge. If a quest name or step is too long to fit, it gets trimmed with "..." so it stays inside the box, and you can hover it to read the whole thing.

1.4.0
- New: an opacity slider for the pinned quests box on screen. If the pinned quests in the corner feel too in-your-face, you can now turn them down so they fade into the background instead of turning them off completely. It's in the settings under Appearance, "Pinned quests opacity". Drag it down to make the whole box (the panel and the text) more see-through.
- New: hover a pinned quest to see all its steps. Some quests have more than one step, but the box on screen only has room to show the first one. Now you can rest your mouse on a pinned quest and a little popup shows every step still left to do, so you can check what's coming without opening the journal. There's a setting to turn this off ("Show all steps on hover").
- New: quests with more than one objective line now show all of them in the details panel, not just the first. Before, only multi-step More Quests quests got this; now plain quests that list several things to do show every line.
- Fixed: quests that only ask you to reach a friendship level were showing up as Failed in the Completed tab even after you finished them. They now show as done.

1.3.0
- New: collect a quest's gold reward right from the journal. When you finish a quest by playing it (like delivering an item or catching fish), the game leaves the gold waiting for you to pick up. Before, you had to open the old quest log to grab it. Now the finished quest shows up at the top of the Active tab with a little gold coin next to its name and a Claim Reward button. Click it and the gold is yours, with a little popup telling you how much, and the quest moves to the Completed tab. Special Orders are covered too: a finished order with gold waiting shows at the top of the Special Orders tab with the same coin and Claim Reward button. Grabbing it from the old quest log still works too.
- New: opening the journal stops the flashing "!" on the game's quest button. When you get a new quest the game flashes a "!" until you open the old quest log. Since you use this journal instead, opening it now counts as checking, so the flashing stops. There's a setting to turn this off if you want to keep it. A quest that's finished but still owes you gold keeps flashing until you collect, on purpose.

1.2.0
- Fixed the Esc-menu tab clashing with other menu mods. If you had a mod like UI Info Suite 2 (and not Better Game Menu), the Quest Journal tab could land right on top of that mod's tab, so clicking it opened the journal instead of the other mod, and in some cases the game froze. The Esc-menu tab now only shows up when you have Better Game Menu installed. Without it, open the journal with the F6 key or the Iconic Framework icon like before.
- New: sort the quest list. There's a "Sort" dropdown at the top left. Order by deadline (soonest due first, no-deadline quests at the bottom), by name (A to Z), by who gave the quest, by where it came from, or by category. Your choice is remembered and used on every tab. You can also set the starting order in the settings.
- New: filter your custom tabs by deadline. When you make or edit a tab there's now a Deadline box. Type a number for quests with exactly that many days left, "<=5" for 5 days or fewer, "None" for quests with no deadline, a range like "1-3", or a comparison like ">28". Mix and match with commas: "<=3, !2" means 3 days or less except exactly 2, and "None, >28" means no deadline or more than 28 days out. Covers special orders too.
- New: filters can match several things at once and exclude things. Separate words with commas to match any of them, and start a word with "!" to leave it out. So "RSV, More Quests" in Source shows quests from either mod, and "!Robin, crop order" in Title shows crop order quests that aren't from Robin. The Kind box also ignores spaces, so "!Special Orders" hides all special orders.
- The make-a-tab window is tidier now. Instead of a wall of text, each box has a small "?" next to it. Hover the "?" to see what that box does and an example of what to type.
- Fixed the pinned quest box on screen. If you changed your UI scale away from 100%, clicking a pinned quest could open the wrong one or do nothing. Clicks now land on the quest you actually clicked at any UI scale.
- The "days left" line now tells you the real deadline. A quest or special order is removed the morning after its last day, so the journal now says "Final day!" on the day you have to finish it, "Due tomorrow!" when the deadline is the next day, and counts down the real days after that. Heads up: this reads one lower than the vanilla number on purpose (the game's "2 days" is really "due tomorrow"), because that's how much time you actually have. Works the same for regular quests and special orders now.

1.1.0
- Added a journal icon for Iconic Framework. Click it to open the journal.
- Works with a controller now. If you have Star Control, the journal shows up in its radial menu so you can open it with your gamepad.
- Switch between tabs with a controller using the shoulder buttons (LB/RB) or the triggers (LT/RT).
- Edit one of your own custom tabs with a controller: select it, then press Y to open its editor.
- Make a new custom tab with a controller by pressing X.
- Open a pinned quest from the HUD with a controller: point at it with the right stick and press A.
- The controller buttons (tab switching and edit) can be rebound in the settings.

1.0.0
- First release.
- Replaces the plain quest log with a full journal: a quest list on the left, all the details in the middle, and action buttons on the right.
- Cancel or postpone a quest right from the journal. There's also an optional Complete button (off by default) that finishes a quest and pays its reward.
- Multi-step quests from More Quests Framework show each step and how far along it is.
- Special Orders get their own tab.
- Pin quests to a top-right corner of the screen so you can see your current goal while you play. Click a pin to jump straight to that quest.
- Make your own tabs that filter quests by title, source, category, or kind. Saved per save file.
- Search bar at the top to filter the quest list by title.
- Optional warp helper warps you next to a quest's NPC.
- Optional item helper hands you the item a quest asks for.
- Resize the whole window in the settings.
- Open it with F6 or a tab in the Esc menu. Needs StardewUI. More Quests Framework and Generic Mod Config Menu are optional.
