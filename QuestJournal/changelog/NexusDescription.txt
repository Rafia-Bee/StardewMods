[size=4][b]What does this mod do?[/b][/size]

The vanilla quest log is one plain scroll: a title, one line of objective, a reward, and that's it. No way to see the steps of a longer quest, no button to finish or drop a quest, no label saying which mod a quest came from, and nothing on your screen reminding you what you're working on.

Quest Journal replaces that log with a proper journal. A quest list down the left, all the details in the middle, and buttons on the right. It works on its own with vanilla quests, and grows extra features when [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/46361]More Quests Framework[/url] is installed.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Full details panel[/b] - title, description, current objective, rewards, who gave it to you, days left, and which mod it came from[/*]
[*][b]Honest deadlines[/b] - the days-left line tells you the real deadline. A quest or special order is removed the morning after its last day, so the journal says "Final day!" on the day you have to finish it and "Due tomorrow!" when the deadline is the next day. This reads one lower than the game's own number on purpose (the game's "2 days" is really "due tomorrow"), because that's how much time you actually have[/*]
[*][b]Action buttons[/b] - drop a quest you don't want, or push a quest's deadline back a week so it doesn't run out on you[/*]
[*][b]Claim your reward[/b] - when you finish a quest by playing it, the gold waits for you to pick up. The finished quest shows at the top of the Active tab with a little gold coin next to its name and a Claim Reward button. Click it to take the gold (with a popup telling you how much), and the quest moves to the Completed tab. Special Orders work the same way, with their gold claimable from the Special Orders tab. No more opening the old quest log just to grab your pay[/*]
[*][b]Stops the flashing "!"[/b] - when you get a new quest the game flashes a "!" on its quest button until you open the old quest log. Since you use this journal instead, opening it counts as checking, so the flashing stops. Can be turned off in the settings. A quest that's done but still owes you gold keeps flashing until you collect[/*]
[*][b]Multi-step quests[/b] - the longer quests from More Quests Framework show every step and how far along each one is, instead of squashing it into one sentence[/*]
[*][b]Special Orders[/b] - the board quests get their own tab with their rewards listed out[/*]
[*][b]Pin to the screen[/b] - pin any quest and its current goal shows up in the top-right corner while you play. Click a pinned quest to jump straight to it, or hover it to see all the steps still left to do (handy for quests with more than one step). Drag the panel wherever you want it, and fade it down with an opacity slider if it's in the way[/*]
[*][b]Your own tabs[/b] - build tabs that filter the list by title, source mod, category, kind, or deadline. Each box takes more than one word: separate them with commas to match any of them ("RSV, More Quests" in Source shows quests from either), and start a word with "!" to leave it out ("!Robin, crop order" in Title shows crop order quests not from Robin). The Deadline box takes a number (exactly that many days left), "<=5" (5 or fewer), "None", a range like "1-3", a comparison like ">28", or a mix like "<=3, !2". Stored per-save.[/*]
[*][b]Sort the list[/b] - a Sort dropdown at the top left orders by deadline (soonest first), name, giver, source, or category. Your choice is remembered and used on every tab[/*]
[*][b]Search bar[/b] - type in the box at the top to filter the quest list by title[/*]
[*][b]Complete button[/b] (optional, off by default) - a button that finishes a quest and pays its reward without doing the objective. It's a shortcut, so you turn it on yourself[/*]
[*][b]Warp helper[/b] (optional, off by default) - a button that warps you right next to a quest's NPC. If a quest involves more than one NPC you get a little picker[/*]
[*][b]Item helper[/b] (optional, off by default) - a button that hands you the item a quest is asking for so you still do the real turn-in, or bumps a fishing quest's catch count[/*]
[*][b]Resize it[/b] - make the whole window bigger or smaller to taste[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]How to open it[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Press [b]F6[/b] (you can change this key in the settings)[/*]
[*]If you have Better Game Menu, click the [b]Quest Journal[/b] tab in the Esc menu[/*]
[*]If you have Iconic Framework, click the [b]journal icon[/b] on your toolbar[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Playing with a controller[/b][/size]
The journal works with a gamepad. Once it's open you can move around it with the stick or d-pad, just like the game's own menus. To switch between tabs, use the shoulder buttons (LB/RB) or the triggers (LT/RT). To make a new custom tab, press X; to edit the custom tab you have selected, press Y. All of these can be rebound in the settings.

If you have quests pinned to the HUD, you can open one with a controller too: point at it with the right stick and press A.

To open it with a controller, the easiest way is [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/25257]Star Control[/url]. If you have it, the journal shows up in its radial menu (this works through Iconic Framework, so you'll want both installed).

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
If you have [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/5098]Generic Mod Config Menu[/url] installed, a settings page shows up in-game. Otherwise edit the [font=Courier New]config.json[/font] in the mod's folder.
[list]
[*][b]Open journal key[/b] - the hotkey that opens the journal (default F6)[/*]
[*][b]Controller buttons[/b] - while the journal is open: previous/next tab (default shoulders and triggers), new tab (default X), and edit the selected custom tab (default Y). All rebindable[/*]
[*][b]Add a game-menu tab[/b] - adds the Quest Journal tab to the Esc menu. Only works with Better Game Menu installed (without it the tab is left off so it doesn't clash with other menu mods). Needs a restart[/*]
[*][b]Show the Completed tab[/b] and [b]Show the All tab[/b] - turn either off to hide that tab from the journal if you only want to see the quests you're working on. Both on by default[/*]
[*][b]Show pinned quests on the HUD[/b] - draws your pinned quests in the top-right corner[/*]
[*][b]Show all steps on hover[/b] - some quests have more than one step, but the HUD only shows the first. Rest the mouse on a pinned quest to see all the steps still left to do in a little popup, without opening the journal[/*]
[*][b]Auto-pin new quests[/b] - pins a quest the moment you accept it (does nothing if Show pinned quests on the HUD is false)[/*]
[*][b]Journal size[/b] - makes the whole window bigger or smaller[/*]
[*][b]Pinned quests opacity[/b] - how see-through the pinned quests panel is on the HUD. Turn it down to let it fade into the background[/*]
[*][b]Complete button[/b], [b]Item helper[/b], and [b]Warp helper[/b] - the optional cheat buttons, all off by default[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Known limitations[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Lookup Anything[/b] support isn't in yet. Lookup Anything doesn't give other mods a way to open its lookups, so that button is parked until it does.[/*]
[*][b]Bigger size doesn't grow the text.[/b] Scaling the window gives the text more room but keeps it at normal size, because scaling the font itself breaks word-wrap and clicking.[/*]
[*][b]Multiplayer:[/b] pins and custom tabs are saved per player but aren't synced between players. Tested on single-player.[/*]
[/list]
