[size=4][b]What does this mod do?[/b][/size]

Adds a big batch of new quests. They scale with your skills, react to what your character has done, and pull in NPCs and festivals from Ridgeside, East Scarp, Mount Vapius, SVE, and more.

This mod is just the quests. The behind-the-scenes parts (the bigger Help Wanted board, the rules for when quests show up, the rewards, the NPC reactions) live in [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/46361]More Quests Framework[/url], which you need to install alongside this mod.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]70+ new quests[/b] across Farming, Fishing, Mining, Foraging, Animal, Cooking, Festival, Seasonal, and Social categories[/*]
[*][b]Help Wanted board overhaul[/b] - several quests per day instead of vanilla's one[/*]
[*][b]New Adventurer's Guild board[/b] at the mine entrance (opt-out in settings). Mining and combat quests live here so the Help Wanted board doesn't get crowded[/*]
[*][b]Festival prep quests[/b] - mail-delivered helper quests around most vanilla festivals plus Ridgeside Gathering and East Scarp's Spirit's Eve[/*]
[*][b]Animal quests[/b] - Some unique one-time quests from Marnie when you build your first barn/coop. If you have my [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/44349]Livestock Follows You[/url] mod, Leah will give you periodic quests that rewards you with a random animal painting each time (forgive me if the painting assets are bad, I have 0 artistic talent and just reused vanilla sprites)[/*]
[*][b]Cooking quests[/b] - If you have [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/6830]Love of Cooking[/url] or [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/22681]YACS[/url] installed and have "Difficulty Scaling" enabled in configs, cooking related quests will scale with your cooking skill. Villagers actually react to the dish you cooked (loved / liked / hated)[/*]
[*][b]NPC reactions[/b] - some quests (Massive Harvest, Medium Fishing Haul, Seafood Night, Monster Parts, Saloon Specials) make villagers say something based on what you brought in. The line shows up the next time you talk to them[/*]
[*][b]Deep-dive quests[/b] - reach a target floor in the Mines or Skull Cavern and ship a Mining-scaled ore haul[/*]
[*][b]The Unseen Offering[/b] - a magician NPC (the Wizard, or modded magic folk like Lance, Vael, Ivaras, or Alecto) marks a glowing ritual circle out in the wild or on one of the Mines' reward floors, with a tall beam of light so you can spot it from a distance. Bring any poisonous food (Void Mayonnaise, Pufferfish, Red Mushroom, Sea Cucumber, and the like, modded items count too) and leave it inside the ring as an offering to the things that share the valley. A combat-boosting food turns up in the mail the next morning. Needs Combat 5 and Ginger Island unlocked; the Mines only come up once you've reached floor 120, so the elevator can take you straight to the marked floor[/*]
[*][b]Archaeology and museum quests[/b] - Gunther (or his friendable SVE counterpart, or East Scarp's Jasper) asks for a quick dig run on the daily board. With [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/22199]Archaeology Skill[/url] installed the count scales off that skill and pays in Hardwood Displays, otherwise it scales off Mining and pays in geodes / troves. With [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/3753]Stardew Valley Expanded[/url] installed, Gunther also occasionally asks in conversation for a single museum donation and hands you a random museum display piece as thanks[/*]
[*][b]Quality-aware deliveries[/b] - Premium Crop Order (Iridium only), Quality Crop Delivery (Silver+), Quality Fish Delivery (Gold+)[/*]
[*][b]Recipe-gated quests[/b] - Preserves Jar, Keg, Dehydrator, and Fish Smoker requests arrive in the mail the day after you learn the recipe. These are one-time quests.[/*]
[*][b]Works with modded NPCs[/b] - Ridgeside, East Scarp, Mount Vapius, SVE, Eli and Dylan, Arumi the Actress, Sen (Lurking in the Dark), and Gunnar (Bear Fam) NPCs join the quest-giver and reaction pools automatically. I tried my best to exclude non-canon npcs (like The Emperor / Lexi / Krobus / Sen non-human npcs) from the quests, but if some leak through please let me know. Child NPCs (Jas, Vincent, Leo, and any modded child NPCs you've met) are excluded from most boards but they do get one quest of their own: Feed Wild Critters, a small foraging errand. Finish three of those and Marnie gives you a discount on your next pet.[/*]
[*][b]Per-quest toggles[/b] - every quest can be turned off on its own through GMCM[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
If you have [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/5098]Generic Mod Config Menu[/url] installed, two settings pages show up in-game. Otherwise edit the [font=Courier New]config.json[/font] files in each mod's folder.
[list]
[*][b]MoreQuests config[/b] - per-quest weights and toggles, animal quest master toggle, Adventurer's Guild board on/off, festival prep toggles, shop discount sizes, fish haul quantities, Skull Cavern max floor, secret-gift hint on/off, and per-quest knobs.[/*]
[*][b]MoreQuestsFramework config[/b] - engine-level stuff: how many quests show up per day, deadline lengths, gold tiers, friendship reward sizes, consequence grace window, vanilla quest type weights.[/*]
[/list]
Both pages also have an [b]Advanced > Debug logging[/b] toggle at the bottom. Leave it off for normal play, flip it on if you hit a bug and need to share a SMAPI log.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install SMAPI and SpaceCore.[/*]
[*]Download [b]More Quests Framework[/b] and unzip it into your [font=Courier New]Stardew Valley/Mods[/font] folder.[/*]

You can install or remove quests mid-playthrough. Save data only stores currently active quests. If you face any issues, please let me know!

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Multiplayer:[/b] untested. Tested on single-player only.[/*]
[*][b]Not compatible with any other Help Wanted mod, or any mod that directly modifies the Help Wanted board.[/b][/*]
[*][b]Mail Services Mod:[/b] in-person delivery quests work as expected. Mailing items through MSM gives the recipient an extra full heart on top of the quest reward (MSM mirrors vanilla's flat 250-friendship-point delivery bonus regardless of the quest's actual reward). The Forage with Linus quest also doesn't tick when items are mailed because MSM skips vanilla's gift hook. Hand-deliver for the right behaviour.[/*]
[*][b]Friendship-decay / friendship-clamp mods (CJB Cheats Menu's "No friendship decay", [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/22922]No Friendship Decay - Reborn[/url] etc.):[/b] block the negative side of consequence reactions on Massive Harvest, Weekly Specials, Medium Fishing Haul, Seafood Night, and Monster Parts. The positive friendship side still works. Turn that option off if you want the full consequence aspect of the quests.[/*]
[/list]
