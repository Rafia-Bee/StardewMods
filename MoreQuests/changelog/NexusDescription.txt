[size=4][b]What does this mod do?[/b][/size]

Adds a big batch of new quests. They scale with your skills, react to what your character has done, and pull in NPCs and festivals from Ridgeside, East Scarp, Mount Vapius, SVE, and more.

This mod is just the quests. The behind-the-scenes parts (the bigger Help Wanted board, the rules for when quests show up, the rewards, the NPC reactions) live in [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/46361]More Quests Framework[/url], which you need to install alongside this mod.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]80+ new quests[/b] across Farming, Fishing, Mining, Foraging, Animal, Cooking, Festival, Seasonal, and Social categories (full list further down)[/*]
[*][b]Help Wanted board overhaul[/b] - several quests per day instead of vanilla's one[/*]
[*][b]New Adventurer's Guild board[/b] at the mine entrance (opt-out in settings). Mining and combat quests live here so the Help Wanted board doesn't get crowded[/*]
[*][b]Cooking quests scale and villagers taste-test[/b] - If you have [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/6830]Love of Cooking[/url] or [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/22681]YACS[/url] installed and have "Difficulty Scaling" enabled in configs, cooking related quests will scale with your cooking skill. Villagers actually react to the dish you cooked (loved / liked / hated)[/*]
[*][b]NPC reactions[/b] - some quests (Massive Harvest, Medium Fishing Haul, Seafood Night, Monster Parts, Saloon Specials) make villagers say something based on what you brought in. The line shows up the next time you talk to them[/*]
[*][b]Works with modded NPCs[/b] - Ridgeside, East Scarp, Mount Vapius, SVE, Eli and Dylan, Arumi the Actress, Sen (Lurking in the Dark), and Gunnar (Bear Fam) NPCs join the quest-giver and reaction pools automatically. I tried my best to exclude non-canon npcs (like The Emperor / Lexi / Krobus / Sen non-human npcs) from the quests, but if some leak through please let me know. Child NPCs (Jas, Vincent, Leo, and any modded child NPCs you've met) are excluded from most boards but they do get one quest of their own: Feed Wild Critters, a small foraging errand. Finish three of those and Marnie gives you a discount on your next pet.[/*]
[*][b]Per-quest toggles[/b] - every quest can be turned off on its own through GMCM[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Quests[/b][/size]
Cooldowns are tunable in the framework config. The defaults are: [b]Short = 2 days[/b], [b]Medium = 7 days[/b], [b]Long = 14 days[/b]. A quest just says "Short", "Medium", or "Long" unless it uses a fixed number. Quests are grouped by category and listed alphabetically within each group. Click a category to expand its list.

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
If you have [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/5098]Generic Mod Config Menu[/url] installed, two settings pages show up in-game. Otherwise edit the [font=Courier New]config.json[/font] files in each mod's folder.
[list]
[*][b]MoreQuests config[/b] - per-quest weights and toggles, animal quest master toggle, Adventurer's Guild board on/off, festival prep toggles, shop discount sizes, fish haul quantities, Skull Cavern max floor, secret-gift hint on/off, and per-quest knobs.[/*]
[*][b]MoreQuestsFramework config[/b] - engine-level stuff: how many quests show up per day, deadline lengths, gold tiers, friendship reward sizes, consequence grace window, vanilla quest type weights.[/*]
[/list]
Both pages also have an [b]Advanced > Debug logging[/b] toggle at the bottom. Leave it off for normal play, flip it on if you hit a bug and need to share a SMAPI log.
