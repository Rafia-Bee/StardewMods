To be released
- New Joja questline. Once you've earned a bit of money, Morris from JojaMart writes to you, and what looks like a simple "prove yourself" test turns into a quiet plan to make Pierre's shop look bad. Win Morris over, sneak a pile of cheap junk produce onto Pierre's shelves, then sit back while the whole town grumbles about it for a few days. Nobody ever finds out it was you. It only shows up while the Community Center is still standing, since Morris leaves town once it's finished.
- New "Don't get caught" quest, Pierre's turn to get back at Joja. Once you're friendly enough with him, Pierre asks you to sneak into JojaMart after midnight (the door only opens for you between midnight and 2am), pack the shelves with the cheapest pickled rice and wheat you can find, put up a "sale" sign out front with a pickle on it, then go home and act normal. For a few days after, the town jokes about Morris and his sad pickle sale while Pierre quietly enjoys it, and the sign you put up comes down once the talk dies off. Like the rest of it, nobody ever pins it on you. Only shows up while the Community Center is still standing.
- New setting for that questline: you can change how cheap a crop has to be to count as junk for the sabotage step. Raise it if you run mods that make crops worth more, so you can still find something cheap enough to sell.

2.4.0
- Needs More Quests Framework 2.6.0 or newer.
- New "Clear the floor" quest on the Adventurer's Guild board: a combat villager asks you to put down a batch of monsters across a stretch of mine floors. It picks floors you've already been to, or the Skull Cavern once you've unlocked it, and finishing it earns you a Glow Ring.
- New "Batten down the hatches" quest: when a storm is on the way, a villager mails you a heads-up and asks you to get some lightning rods up on the farm before it hits. Put up enough of them and a battery pack turns up in your mailbox the next morning. Only comes up if you've learned how to craft lightning rods.
- New "Know your waters" fishing quest: an angler asks you to catch one of every kind of fish that lives at one spot this season, the whole set, nothing skipped. Once you've caught them all, go talk to them and they'll ask which fish gave you the most trouble. How you answer changes your reward, and being humble about it pays off best. It shows up on the help-wanted board in the first few days of a season so you have the whole season to finish.
- New "Redecorate a Home" quest: a villager posts a job asking you to spruce up their place. They hand you a budget up front, you go buy furniture (lamps, rugs, chairs, tables) and set it up inside their home, and whatever you don't spend goes back to them. You only ever pay the overspend out of pocket. Finish it and they thank you with a handful of their favorite gifts. This was its own little "Architect" mod before, now it's just part of More Quests. The quest only shows up if you have Build Placement Unlocker installed, since that's what lets you put furniture in someone else's house.
- Beach cleanup quests now tell you how many shells to collect, like "Collect 4 Cockle and talk to Elliott." They used to just say "Collect Cockle" with no number, so it was easy to come up short.
- Handing shells to the quest giver before you have enough no longer gives them away as a gift. They'll just let you know how many more to bring.

2.2.0
- Needs More Quests Framework 2.3.0 or newer.
- New "Emily's Housewarming Challenge" quest: reach 5 hearts with Emily and she mails you a challenge to liven up your farmhouse. Place a rug, a light source, a wall decoration, and a few more pieces of furniture, then tell her it's ready. Only furniture you put down while the quest is going counts, so moving things around won't cheat it. Emily and Robin send you a surprise dresser, plus a friendship boost. You can change how many pieces it asks for in the settings.
- New "The Unseen Offering" quest on the Adventurer's Guild board: a magic NPC marks a glowing ritual circle (look for the beam of light) out in the wild or deep in the Mines. Leave spoiled, inedible food inside the ring and a combat food turns up in the mail next morning. Needs Combat 5 and Ginger Island.
- New Crop Cycle quest: a farmer NPC asks you to grow a crop start to finish. Sow seeds, water them, harvest the crop, then deliver the haul. 28-day deadline. Pays gold plus a stack of Hyper Speed-Gro. The crop pick respects how many days are left in the current season, so you won't get rolled a crop you can't finish in time. Sprinklers, rain, and Junimo Huts all count toward the watering and harvesting steps.
- Coal Point Farm and Ripley NPCs join the quest-giver pools.
- The Adventurer's Guild board now shows up as soon as you can reach the mine entrance instead of waiting until Spring 14, and its default spot moved a little lower on the wall.
- Quests no longer ask for crops, gems, or flowers you can't reasonably get yet. Qi crops, Prismatic Shard, and the Galdoran Gem are never requested. Ancient Fruit, Sweet Gem Berry, and the Ginger Island and Desert crops (Pineapple, Taro Root, Starfruit, and so on) won't be asked for during your first year.

2.0.0
- Updated for the new framework. Requires More Quests Framework 2.0.0 or newer (older framework versions are incompatible, please update both at the same time).
- No new quests or gameplay changes in this version; under the hood, the mod now hands its quests to the framework through Content Patcher's asset system instead of the old loader, which makes it future-proof for any third-party CP packs that want to add or tweak quests.
- GMCM edits to the Short / Medium / Long cooldown days now take effect the same day, no save reload needed.
- Cooking quest scaling now detects Love of Cooking too, not just YACS. Either mod (or neither) works.
- Furniture shipping quest descriptions now include a small in-character hint about where to source the items (Marnie sells hay bales, Robin's catalogue rotates tables, etc.) so players aren't left guessing.
- Legendary Fish quest fixed: only picks fish that are actually in season (Angler in winter was a known case), never picks Mr. Qi's extended-family legendaries (Ms. Angler, Son of Crimsonfish, Glacierfish Jr., Radioactive Carp, Legend II), skips legendaries you've already caught, and won't repeat the same fish back-to-back.
