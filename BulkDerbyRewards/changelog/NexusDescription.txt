[size=4][b]What does this mod do?[/b][/size]

This mod lets you exchange all your Golden Tags in a single interaction and collect every reward from a chest-style menu, the same way the SquidFest works.

Walk up to the derby booth, and if you're carrying more than one Golden Tag, the mod asks: "Exchange all X tags for prizes?" Say yes, and your rewards appear in a grab menu. Take what you want, close when you're done.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Bulk exchange[/b]: Turn in all Golden Tags at once instead of one by one[/*]
[*][b]Chest-style menu[/b]: Browse and collect prizes at your own pace[/*]
[*][b]Vanilla prize cycle[/b]: Prizes follow the same rotation as the base game (Tent Kit first, then the standard cycle)[/*]
[*][b]Uncollected items drop safely[/b]: Close the menu early and leftover prizes drop at your feet[/*]
[*][b]Configurable[/b]: Toggle the mod on/off or force bulk mode even for a single tag[/*]
[/list]
[size=4][b]
How to use[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Fish during the [b]Trout Derby[/b] (Summer 20–21) and collect Golden Tags as usual.[/*]
[*]Visit the [b]prize booth[/b] with your tags.[/*]
[*]If you have multiple tags, the mod asks if you want to exchange them all at once.[/*]
[*]Confirm, and a [b]chest menu[/b] opens with all your prizes.[/*]
[*]Grab the items you want. Anything left behind when you close the menu is dropped on the ground.[/*]
[/list]
[size=4][b]
Configuration[/b][/size]
If you have [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/5098]Generic Mod Config Menu[/url] installed, you can tweak these from the in-game options. Otherwise, edit [font=Courier New]config.json[/font] in the mod folder.
[list]
[*][b]Enabled[/b] (default: Yes). Turn the mod on or off entirely. When off, the vanilla one-at-a-time exchange is used.[/*]
[*][b]Always Bulk[/b] (default: No). When enabled, even a single Golden Tag uses the chest-style menu instead of the vanilla exchange.[/*]
[/list]

FAQ[/b][/size]
[b]Can I install this mid-playthrough?[/b]
Yes! It works immediately at the next Trout Derby.
[b]Does this change the prizes I get?[/b]
No. The prize cycle is the same as vanilla. The mod just lets you collect them all at once instead of one at a time.
[b]What if my inventory is full?[/b]
Any prizes you can't pick up stay in the chest menu. If you close the menu with items still in it, they drop on the ground next to you.
[b]Does this work with the SquidFest too?[/b]
No need - the SquidFest already has a bulk reward system built into the vanilla game. This mod brings that same experience to the Trout Derby.
