[size=4][b]What does this mod do?[/b][/size]

When you buy an animal, it follows you around instead of teleporting straight to its building. Once you reach the farm and enter the barn or coop, the animal is registered inside and goes back to normal. This only happens on the walk home after purchase -- your existing animals won't start following you around.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Follow the leader[/b] -- Purchased animals trail behind you as you walk[/*]
[*][b]Grazing Bell[/b] -- Buy the Grazing Bell from Marnie's shop (500g) and click any registered farm animal to take it for a walk. Walking animals graze nearby grass when you stand still, gaining a happiness boost. Send them home by clicking again with the bell (requires 3+ hearts of friendship)[/*]
[*][b]Idle roaming[/b] -- Walking animals don't just stand around. They wander short distances, pause, sit, and eat naturally when you stop moving[/*]
[*][b]NPC reactions[/b] -- Villagers you walk past will comment on your animals with speech bubbles. 131 unique lines, from sweet to snarky. Marnie, Shane, and Jas have their own personality-driven dialog, and reactions change based on weather[/*]
[*][b]Outdoor stall support[/b] -- Works with outdoor animal vendors too (e.g. Moira's Glimsap Fair stall from Visit Mount Vapius); animals appear right away[/*]
[*][b]Livestock Bazaar compatible[/b] -- Hooks into the standard adoption path, so any mod that routes through it is supported[/*]
[*][b]Farm arrival[/b] -- When you reach the farm, animals spread out in front of their barn or coop and wait for you to enter the building[/*]
[*][b]Auto-delivery[/b] -- Animals are delivered to their buildings when you walk inside, or automatically at a configurable curfew time (default 8 PM)[/*]
[*][b]Sprint to catch up[/b] -- Animals that fall behind sprint back to you. Only animals that fall really far behind (past twice the catch-up distance) teleport[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
If you have [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/5098]Generic Mod Config Menu[/url] installed, all settings are available in-game. Otherwise, edit [font=Courier New]config.json[/font] in the mod folder.
[list]
[*][b]Enabled[/b] (default: Yes) -- Toggle the mod on or off; when off, animals teleport to barns as usual[/*]
[*][b]Follow Speed[/b] (default: 1.0) -- Speed multiplier for catch-up when animals fall behind[/*]
[*][b]Catch-up Distance[/b] (default: 10 tiles) -- How far behind an animal can fall before it sprints to catch up. If it falls behind by more than twice this distance, it teleports to you[/*]
[*][b]Auto-deliver Time[/b] (default: 8 PM) -- Game time when undelivered animals are sent home automatically[/*]
[*][b]Animal Sounds[/b] (default: Yes) -- Whether animals make sounds while following[/*]
[*][b]Sound Interval[/b] (default: 15s) -- Seconds between animal sounds[/*]
[*][b]Show Notifications[/b] (default: Yes) -- HUD messages for follow and delivery events[/*]
[*][b]NPC Reactions[/b] (default: Yes) -- Nearby villagers react with speech bubbles when you escort animals[/*]
[*][b]Debug Logging[/b] (default: No) -- Log extra info to the SMAPI console for troubleshooting[/*]
[*][b]Grazing Happiness[/b] (default: 15) -- Happiness gained per grass eaten during a walk[/*]
[*][b]Send Home Friendship[/b] (default: 750 / 3 hearts) -- Friendship needed to send a walking animal home alone[/*]
[*][b]Grazing Idle Time[/b] (default: 2s) -- How long you need to stand still before animals start grazing[/*]
[/list]

[b]Note for Livestock Bazaar users[/b]
When buying animals with Livestock Bazaar installed, you may see [font=Courier New]Failed to find valid animal[/font] warnings in the SMAPI console. This is harmless and happens because the mod moves the animal out of its building before Livestock Bazaar finishes processing. The animal is safe and will follow you as expected.
