# Stardew Mods

A collection of my mods for [Stardew Valley](https://www.stardewvalley.net/).

## Mods

| Mod | What it does | Download |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bigger Auto-Grabber](BiggerAutoGrabber/) | Lets the auto-grabber hold a lot more than the usual 36 slots. | [Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/43013) |
| [Bulk Derby Rewards](BulkDerbyRewards/) | Turn in all your Golden Tags at once during the Trout Derby and grab the rewards from one menu instead of one by one. | [Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/43009) |
| [Catch of the Day](CatchOfTheDay/) | Shows icons on your HUD for fish you can only catch in certain weather (rain, storm, sun, snow, wind, green rain). Hover to see where and when they bite. | [Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/43668) |
| [Deluxe Grabber Fix](DeluxeGrabberFix/) | A fixed-up version of Deluxe Grabber for Stardew 1.6. The auto-grabber can pull in crops, fruit trees, bushes, forage, slime balls, and artifact spots. | [Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/43136) |
| [Livestock Follows You](LivestockFollowsYou/) | New animals you buy walk home behind you instead of popping straight into the barn. After that they act normal. | [Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/44349) |
| [Loanable Tractor](LoanableTractor/) | Rent a tractor from Joja for a small daily fee, no garage needed. Needs Tractor Mod. | [Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/42931) |
| [More Quests](MoreQuests/) | Adds over 70 new quests of all kinds. Built on More Quests Framework. | [Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/46362) |
| [More Quests Framework](MoreQuestsFramework/) | A framework for mod makers to add their own quests, plus controls for how often vanilla Help Wanted quests show up. | [Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/46361) |
| [Quest Journal](QuestJournal/) | A nicer quest journal. See each step of a quest, pin quests to your screen, complete or cancel them, and more. Works great with More Quests Framework. | [Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/46966) |
| [Smelt Rings](SmeltLuckyRings/) | Throw any vanilla ring into the furnace and get back ore, gems, or crafting bits. | [Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/46258) |
