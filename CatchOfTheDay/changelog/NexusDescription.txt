[size=4][b]What does this mod do?[/b][/size]

Shows weather-exclusive fish as small icons on the right side of your screen. At a glance you can see which special fish are available today, where to catch them, and when.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Weather-exclusive fish on the HUD[/b] -- Rain, storm, sun, snow, wind, and green rain fish all tracked[/*]
[*][b]Custom time slots[/b] -- Define up to 5 time ranges (e.g., 6-12, 18-2) to show fish that only spawn during those hours, each with its own background color[/*]
[*][b]Hide individual fish[/b] -- Press a keybind (default: Delete) while hovering a fish icon to hide it from the HUD; manage hidden fish in the config menu[/*]
[*][b]Hover tooltips[/b] -- See the fish name, spawn locations you've visited, and catchable time windows[/*]
[*][b]Bundle tracking[/b] -- Tooltips show which Community Center bundles still need each fish[/*]
[*][b]Uncaught indicator[/b] -- Fish you haven't caught yet are labeled "(Uncaught)" in the tooltip[/*]
[*][b]Lookup Anything support[/b] -- Hover a fish icon and press F1 to open its Lookup Anything page[/*]
[*][b]Modded fish and locations[/b] -- Automatically picks up fish from SVE, East Scarp, Mount Vapius, and any mod using standard spawn data[/*]
[*][b]Configurable background colors[/b] -- Tint fish catchable right now or matching a time slot with custom colors[/*]
[*][b]Per-weather toggles[/b] -- Enable or disable each weather type individually[/*]
[/list]


[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
If you have [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/5098]Generic Mod Config Menu[/url] installed, all settings are available in-game. Otherwise, edit [font=Courier New]config.json[/font] in the mod folder.
[list]
[*][b]Enable HUD[/b] (default: Yes) -- Show or hide the overlay entirely[/*]
[*][b]Show Bundle Needs[/b] (default: Yes) -- Display Community Center bundle requirements in tooltips[/*]
[*][b]Hide Already Caught[/b] (default: No) -- Filter out fish you've already caught[/*]
[*][b]Minimum Sell Price[/b] (default: 0) -- Only show fish worth at least this much gold[/*]
[*][b]Hide Fish Key[/b] (default: Delete) -- Press while hovering a fish icon to hide it from the HUD[/*]
[*][b]Time Slots[/b] -- Up to 5 custom time ranges with individual colors; fish matching a slot are shown even without a weather requirement[/*]
[*][b]Weather toggles[/b] -- Individual on/off for rain, storm, sun, snow, wind, and green rain[/*]
[*][b]Position and layout[/b] -- Adjustable X/Y position, horizontal or vertical layout, icon scale, and tooltip location limit[/*]
[*][b]Background colors[/b] -- Custom tint for "catchable now" fish and per-slot time colors (requires [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/10505]GMCM Options[/url] for color picker sliders, otherwise uses hex text input)[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
Works with any mod that adds fish through the standard [font=Courier New]Data/Locations[/font] spawn system. Tested on Windows single-player with Stardew Valley 1.6.

This mod supports translations! Please feel free to translate the mod to your language and upload to Nexus on your own page. Nexus will automatically link it to this mod.
